Privacy policy · effective 14 September 2026

rāhī is a personal travel journal: a map of the countries you have been to, the trips that took you there, and the memories you attach to places. This page explains what the app stores, where, and why. It is written to be read, not skimmed past.

What we store, and where

  • Your account. When you sign in with email, Google, or Apple we keep the email address and an account ID. With Google or Apple we never see your password. Apple may give us a private relay address instead of your real email if you choose that.
  • Your travel data. Countries you mark as visited, trips and day-by-day itineraries, memories (a place, a date, a note), and the settings you choose. This is stored in a database hosted by Supabase so it follows you between devices and survives reinstalling the app.
  • Photos. Photos you attach to memories stay in your phone's photo library. The app stores only a reference to the photo and a small 240-pixel thumbnail so the memory can show a picture on the map and in the journal. Full-size photos are not uploaded. If a photo has no library reference, a copy is kept in the app's private storage on your device only.
  • Itineraries you import. If you paste or upload a booking, itinerary, or notes for the app to read, that text is sent to an AI model (Anthropic's Claude, via our own server) to turn it into structured trip days. It is used only to produce your itinerary and is not used to train models.

Services the app talks to

  • Supabase hosts the account system and database.
  • Google Sign-In and Sign in with Apple, only if you choose them to sign in.
  • Anthropic processes text you ask the app to import or edit.
  • OpenFreeMap serves the map tiles and OpenStreetMap Nominatim answers place searches. Like any web request, these see your device's IP address and the map area or search text.
  • Sentry receives crash reports (device model, OS version, app version, and what the app was doing when it crashed) so we can fix bugs.
  • PostHog receives anonymous usage events (for example "trip created", "memory added") tied to your account ID, so we can see which features are used. No message contents, notes, or photos are included.

What we do not do

  • We do not sell or share your data with advertisers, and there are no ads.
  • We do not read your contacts, messages, or location in the background. Location is only what you attach to a memory or trip yourself, or what a photo's own metadata says.
  • We do not use your data to train AI models, and we ask our providers not to either.

Your choices

  • Photo access can be limited to selected photos in your phone's settings; the app works with whatever you allow.
  • Export or delete. Email us and we will send you a copy of your data or delete your account and everything in it. Deletion is completed within 30 days.
  • Sign out at any time from Settings; your data stays in your account until you delete it.

Beta testing

During TestFlight and early Android testing, Apple and Google may additionally collect crash and usage information under their own policies, and builds may change quickly. Test data you enter is real data in your account and is treated as above.

Children

rāhī is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect their data.
